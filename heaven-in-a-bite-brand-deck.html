4. High-Fidelity Website Architecture

The updated public landing page in index.html is designed to load instantly and behave beautifully on mobile and iPad screens. We simplified the layout by removing clunky external slider plugins and heavy code, while keeping the core experience highly engaging.

  • Premium Aesthetics: Styled with Google Fonts "Playfair Display" and "DM Sans", featuring beautiful SVG vector elements matching Koulla's brand.
  • Bespoke Showcase Gallery: Interactive filtering allows customers to explore Celebration Cakes, Wedding Cakes, Savory Platters, or Sweet Treats.
  • Tactile Booking Planner: A fluid pricing calculator that estimates the basket total based on portion metrics.
  • Direct Social Routing: One-tap integration to generate fully filled booking messages, dispatching them directly to Koulla on WhatsApp or E-mail.
  • Elegant FAQ Accordion: Anticipates client questions on logistics, baking schedules, lead times, and EFT billing.
